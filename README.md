# ⚖️ LexiLaw AI – AI-Powered Global Legal Assistance Platform

**LexiLaw AI** is an intelligent, AI-powered legal assistance platform designed to make legal information, rights awareness, and judicial guidance accessible to everyone. By leveraging Large Language Models (LLMs) and Google's Gemini API, the platform helps users understand complex legal concepts, explore legal rights, and obtain structured guidance through a simple natural-language conversational interface.

The platform aims to bridge the gap between ordinary citizens and complex legal systems by transforming complicated legal information into simplified, understandable, and actionable explanations. With a global perspective, jurisdiction-aware assistance, and multilingual capabilities, LexiLaw AI aspires to make legal knowledge more accessible across different countries and linguistic communities.

## 🎯 Project Overview

Understanding legal rights and navigating legal procedures can be challenging due to complicated terminology, limited legal awareness, language barriers, and difficulties finding relevant legal resources.

LexiLaw AI addresses these challenges through an intelligent conversational platform that allows users to describe their legal concerns in everyday language and receive AI-generated explanations, relevant legal information, and guidance on possible next steps.

Rather than manually searching through multiple legal websites and documents, users can interact with an AI assistant to explore legal topics, understand their rights, and discover relevant legal resources.

## 🧠 AI Integration

LexiLaw AI integrates Google's Gemini API through the Google GenAI SDK to provide intelligent natural-language interactions.

The AI component is designed to:
- Understand user-described legal problems.
- Identify the context and category of legal queries.
- Generate simplified legal explanations.
- Provide structured and context-aware responses.
- Assist users in understanding possible legal actions.
- Support conversational exploration of legal topics.

The long-term direction includes integrating Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) with verified legal databases to improve factual grounding, legal references, and jurisdiction-specific accuracy.

## 🏗️ System Architecture

The application follows a modern Single Page Application (SPA) architecture with AI API integration.

**Frontend Layer:** React and TypeScript handle the user interface, application state, and interactive components.

**Styling Layer:** Tailwind CSS provides responsive layouts and consistent visual design.

**Build Layer:** Vite supports fast development and optimized application builds.

**AI Integration Layer:** Google's Gemini API and the Google GenAI SDK provide natural-language processing and intelligent response generation.

**Localization Layer:** Centralized locale management supports language selection and multilingual interface adaptation.

**Voice Interaction Layer:** Web Speech API enables browser-based speech input and related voice functionalities.

**Storage Layer:** Browser LocalStorage supports conversation history and user preferences.
